Colophon & credits

A small personal website inspired by the profile pages and community forums of the early web. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

University identities

University marks identify the institutions in my education history. They belong to their respective universities; this is a personal website, not an official university page.

  • Rice University logo: Rice University Brand Guide.
  • Shanghai Maritime University emblem: 上海海事大学经济管理学院 · 校徽图库.

Activity calendars

GitHub activity comes from my public GitHub contribution calendar. LeetCode activity comes from my LeetCode profile. LeetCode counts are submissions, not unique problems solved.

The calendars show a 365-day snapshot and the date it was retrieved. Activity is refreshed daily when scheduled deployments are enabled. If a service is unavailable, the last successful snapshot is retained. LeetCode timestamps use UTC; platform timezone differences may affect day boundaries.

Travel maps

China province-level geometry: Alibaba Cloud DataV.GeoAtlas. World and United States state geometry: Natural Earth (public domain), via its official data repository. Maps are simplified for a personal travel notebook. Colored areas indicate recorded visits; unmarked areas have no visit recorded.

Privacy

No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Fonts, logos, and activity snapshots are served with this site.
